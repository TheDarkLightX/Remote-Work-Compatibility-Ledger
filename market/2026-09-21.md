## Repeated task primitives this week

Across these roles and contracts, the recurring work is still:

1. define test strategy and acceptance criteria for non-deterministic systems;
2. build high-signal benchmark/golden-set cases rather than simply large test counts;
3. detect regressions, drift, hallucinations, grounding failures, and tool-call errors;
4. combine deterministic checks, model-based judges, and human calibration;
5. design adversarial and synthetic cases that expose meaningful failure modes;
6. capture reproducible failure traces and root-cause hypotheses;
7. distinguish model failure from prompt, data, harness, policy, and evaluator failure;
8. turn quality measurements into release/deployment decisions;
9. support human-in-the-loop escalation when automation is insufficient;
10. maintain reproducible evaluation infrastructure and explicit quality gates.

## Implication for EVAL-001

EVAL-001 already covers most of the machine-side primitives. The remaining career value is concentrated in the human-owned judgment layer:

- personally calibrate the gold labels;
- identify at least one evaluator false positive/insufficiency;
- weigh a slice regression against an improvement instead of trusting the aggregate;
- state a bounded deployment recommendation;
- explain what the benchmark cannot prove;
- complete the oral defense without AI;
- reproduce the committed result from a clean checkout.

## Market conclusion

**No project rotation on 2026-09-21.** Finishing EVAL-001 remains higher-value than starting a new benchmark. The market is asking for exactly the evaluation, QA, regression, calibration, and decision-boundary skills the unfinished human-review phase is designed to demonstrate.
