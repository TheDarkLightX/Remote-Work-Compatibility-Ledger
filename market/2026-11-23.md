## Recurring task primitives

1. Turn ambiguous product quality into explicit, testable criteria, gold labels, and adjudication rules.
2. Check **construct validity**: distinguish a correct grader implementation from a grader that measures the right property.
3. Diagnose regression slices and error severity rather than trusting an aggregate pass rate.
4. Reproduce failures with traceable cases, versioning, deterministic checks, CI, and human calibration.
5. State the evidence boundary and decide whether the available evaluation justifies release or escalation.
