## Recurring task primitives observed

Across the stronger roles, the repeated work is:

1. define measurable quality and acceptance criteria;
2. build or curate eval datasets / golden sets;
3. create automated and semi-automated graders;
4. calibrate model judges against human labels;
5. detect drift, regressions, and slice-specific failures;
6. distinguish model failure from harness/data/prompt failure;
7. reproduce and analyze concrete failure cases;
8. communicate whether evidence supports deployment or further investigation;
9. keep evaluation infrastructure reproducible and reviewable;
10. combine deterministic/rule-based checks with model-based evaluation rather than trusting one opaque score.

## Implication for EVAL-001

EVAL-001 already maps unusually well to these primitives. The highest-value remaining work is therefore **not another machine implementation pass**. It is the human-owned evaluation layer that current roles also emphasize:

- review/correct the gold set;
- inspect disagreement and boundary cases;
- identify evaluator insufficiency;
- reason about conflicting slice movement;
- decide what claim the evidence actually licenses;
- complete the unaided oral defense;
- perform the clean replay and conservative rubric score.

Until those gates are complete, replacing EVAL-001 with another project would reduce portfolio signal-to-noise.

## Market conclusion

**No project rotation this week.** The fresh market evidence increases the expected career value of finishing EVAL-001. The project should graduate only after the human-review, replay, decision, and defense gates are genuinely complete.
